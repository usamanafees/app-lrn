REGULATION (EU) 2019/2144 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL
on type-approval requirements for motor vehicles with respect to their general
safety and the protection of vehicle occupants and vulnerable road users

Document ID: EU-GSR-2019-ANNEX-II
Annex II — Advanced driver assistance systems (excerpt)

================================================================================
SECTION A — GENERAL PROVISIONS
================================================================================

A.1. Vehicles referred to in Article 2(1) shall be equipped with the systems
listed in Section B as from the dates specified in Article 6.

A.2. Systems shall be type-approved in accordance with the corresponding UN
Regulations or technical specifications referenced in column 4 of the table
in Section B.

A.3. Where a UN Regulation is updated, the version in force at the date of
application set out in Article 6 shall apply unless a later version is
mandated by Commission delegated act.

A.4. The manufacturer shall ensure that systems remain operational during normal
operating conditions and shall not design systems to depend on continuous
connectivity to external networks for core safety functions.

SECTION C — PERFORMANCE AND TEST REQUIREMENTS (AEB EXCERPT)
================================================================================

C.1. Advanced emergency braking for pedestrian and cyclist protection

C.1.1. The system shall comply with UN Regulation No. 152 as amended at the
application date for the vehicle category.

C.1.2. In addition, the following EU-specific provisions apply:

C.1.2.1. The warning tell-tale symbol shall conform to ISO 2575 symbol ID P-087
or subsequent revision accepted by the type-approval authority.

C.1.2.2. The system shall remain enabled by default at each ignition cycle.

C.1.2.3. The driver information message displayed upon system intervention shall
be comprehensible without reference to the owner's manual for the initial
warning within a single driving session.

C.1.3. Verification

C.1.3.1. Type-approval testing shall include scenarios AEBS-01 through AEBS-05
as defined in UN R152 Annex 2 or equivalent scenarios accepted by the authority.

C.1.3.2. Test reports shall record ambient illuminance, road surface condition,
tyre specification, and vehicle loading condition.

C.1.3.3. Repeatability: each mandatory scenario shall be executed until pass
criteria are met or the maximum number of runs specified in the test protocol
is exhausted; failures shall be documented with sensor logs.

C.2. Event data recording linkage

C.2.1. Where event data recorders are fitted, AEBS activation events shall be
recorded with a minimum set of data elements:
    - time stamp (UTC, 1 s resolution minimum);
    - vehicle speed;
    - brake pedal status;
    - AEBS state (armed, warning, braking, fault);
    - longitudinal acceleration.

C.2.2. Data elements shall be readable by approved test equipment without
requiring manufacturer proprietary tools unless those tools are supplied to
the approval authority.

C.2.3. Personal identifiers (e.g., VIN-linked driver profiles) shall not be
written to the public EDR dataset accessible during roadside inspection.

================================================================================
SECTION D — CONFORMITY OF PRODUCTION
================================================================================

D.1. The manufacturer shall demonstrate that series-produced vehicles conform
to the approved type with respect to each fitted system in Section B.

D.2. Conformity of production shall include functional checks of:
    (a) tell-tale operation;
    (b) fault detection and indication; and
    (c) absence of diagnostic trouble codes related to safety system faults.

D.3. Sampling frequency and acceptance criteria shall be documented in the
manufacturer's quality management system and made available to the authority
upon request.

D.4. Software updates affecting safety systems shall be notified to the authority
when they alter type-approved behavior unless covered by an prior agreement on
over-the-air update governance.

================================================================================
SECTION E — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS (EXCERPT)
================================================================================

E.1. Vehicles type-approved before 7 July 2022 are exempt from Section B
requirements until major retrofit programs are mandated by member states.

E.2. Small series manufacturers (<= 1,000 units per year) may apply for
extended timelines subject to a documented safety equivalency argument.

E.3. Exemptions shall not apply to systems where UN Regulations are marked
"mandatory without phase-in" in Annex II column 5 (not reproduced in excerpt).
